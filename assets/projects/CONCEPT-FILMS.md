# Original motion studies — Ai FRAHAT

Created locally on 2026-10-24 with original procedural drawing code in
`tools/render-concept-films.py`, Pillow and FFmpeg. No Samsy footage, proprietary
models, stock video, external generation service or downloaded artwork is used.
Typography uses locally installed Arial; the font files are not distributed.

Five silent H.264 videos: 1280 × 720, 24 fps, 12 seconds each, with matching JPEG
posters. Each has three four-second chapters and an explicit concept-film label.

- ATHR: a growing spiral of connected experiences; learn, build, reflect.
- MERATH: three interacting networks, representing human knowledge, method and agent.
- ASL: a moving archive of contextual records.
- WASL: a rotating network globe linking personal skills, human methods and open experience.
- ASLHU: a receding portal reserved for the next experiment, without a finished-product claim.

The other two videos are owner-supplied recordings of AiFRAHAT.com and iMHOTiP.com.
Only the currently selected/nearby screen plays a video; the rest show posters.

Recreate the five concept films with Python + Pillow and FFmpeg on PATH:
`python tools/render-concept-films.py`
